About the Project

Demonstrating how multi-paradigm Artificial Intelligence transforms modern predictive maintenance.

Executive Overview

AI Predictive Maintenance is an intelligent industrial operations platform engineered to forecast machine breakdowns prior to catastrophic operational downtime. Rather than relying on a single estimator, the system orchestrates Supervised Learning (failure prediction), Unsupervised Learning (condition grouping), and Reinforcement Learning (prescriptive decision dispatch).

AI4I 2020 Dataset

10,000 Records

Synthetic milling machine telemetry reflecting real industrial conditions:

Record Volume: 10,000 entries
Attribute Dimensions: 14 columns
Core Features: Air Temp, Process Temp, Speed, Torque, Wear
Failure Modes: TWF, HDF, PWF, OSF, RNF

Triple AI Architecture

1. Supervised Learning Random Forest

Calibrated ensemble classifier estimating binary failure risk and probability metrics.

2. Unsupervised Learning K-Means

Clusters operating regimes into Optimal, Medium, and High-Load mechanical stress states.

3. Reinforcement Learning Q-Learning

Determines cost-optimal policy actions (CONTINUE, INSPECT, MAINTENANCE).
