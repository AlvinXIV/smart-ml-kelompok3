AI Models Architecture

Three distinct machine learning paradigms harmonized into an end-to-end predictive intelligence engine.

Supervised Learning

Random Forest

Ensemble of decision trees trained on labeled historical failures. Identifies complex non-linear failure indicators such as heat dissipation (HDF) and power failure (PWF).

Key Input Features
Type, Air Temp, Process Temp, Speed, Torque, Wear
Primary Output
Failure Risk & Probability (0 - 100%)
Unsupervised Learning

K-Means Clustering

Partitions machines into 3 distinct geometric clusters representing operating severity. Does not require labels and flags anomalous mechanical wear regimes.

Condition Mapping
Cluster 0: Optimal • Cluster 1: Medium • Cluster 2: High Load
Primary Output
Operating Regime Cluster
Reinforcement Learning

Q-Learning Agent

A discrete Markov Decision Process policy that balances downtime inspection penalties against catastrophic equipment loss rewards.

Action Space
CONTINUE • INSPECT • MAINTENANCE
Primary Output
Prescriptive Maintenance Action & Q-Score
